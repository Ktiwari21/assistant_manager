There will be two documents per product, One is Contractual commitments and second is Service description document.
Contractual commitment will have the list of Subscription content, Service Id, Subscription Metrics which defines the 
peak limit for metrics for example 11 million peak order lines, 2500 named users, 6 locations/warehouses, it defines the consumption
limit for mentioned item like order line, warehouse etc. Contractual commitments are non negotiable and are reset on the 1st of every month.
Service description document list the contstraints for various aspect of products such as for categories
enterprise level service volume constraint, Site level service rate constraints and each category will have limit mentioned for
different activity for example, 2 gb egress per month or 45 thousand items per month indicating the constraint at hour level
and some constraint are time bound like every hour only 10 or 100 per minute, kind of time series data and apply at site level. We should show the 
time series data for selected sites in the filter.
Each product will have it's own contractual commitment and service level descriptions and they can have their own
consumption limits on different parameter of usage.
Our aim is to build a kpi dashboard for products which shows the max consumption limit per unit time versus actual consumption.
